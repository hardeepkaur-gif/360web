Why Invest in PPC Advertising?

PPC services make sense when you want measurable enquiries or sales without waiting for organic rankings. You set the budget and pay per click. Every result can be tracked.

Here is what that looks like in practice:

  • Reach people already searching. Someone types “boiler repair Croydon”, and your ad appears. We choose those searches with you.
  • Spend only where you can serve. Why pay to reach people outside your patch? We limit ads to the areas you cover.
  • A budget you control. You set it and can change it as priorities shift. With cost-per-click bidding, you pay only when someone clicks.
  • Every enquiry measured. Once tracking is in place, you can see which clicks led to calls, form fills, or purchases. That tells you what an enquiry really costs.
  • Works alongside SEO. We run PPC alongside SEO, so paid search shows which keywords convert before you chase rankings.

PPC Services FAQs

01 What is a PPC service?

PPC stands for pay-per-click, where you pay only when someone clicks your ad. A PPC service is the expert management of those paid campaigns. We plan, build, track, and optimise your ads. The goal is more qualified enquiries and sales from the same budget.

02 How much do PPC services cost in the UK?

PPC services have two costs: your ad spend and our management fee. Your ad spend goes to Google. For most campaigns, Google won’t charge more than 30.4 times your average daily budget in a month. Our fee depends on your campaigns, platforms, and complexity.

03 What is your minimum contract length?

Our PPC management starts with an X-month initial term. After that, it runs on a rolling monthly basis. You can cancel this contract with X days’ notice. We would rather keep clients through results than long contracts.

04 Is there a minimum ad spend?

We recommend a minimum ad spend of £X per month. Smaller budgets make it harder to test and improve campaigns. Your ad spend goes directly to Google, separate from our fee. We’ll suggest a realistic budget during your free PPC review.

05 Can you take over our existing Google Ads account?

Yes. We link your account to our Google Ads manager account, and its history stays intact. You keep full ownership throughout. We audit the account first. Then we improve what works rather than rebuilding from scratch.

06 Do you work with businesses outside London?

Yes, our team is based in London, but we manage PPC campaigns for businesses across the UK. Everything runs remotely, with regular calls and plain-English monthly reports.

07 Do you offer white-label PPC?

Yes. We manage PPC campaigns for agencies under their own brand. Your clients deal with you, while our specialists run the accounts. Reports can carry your branding.
