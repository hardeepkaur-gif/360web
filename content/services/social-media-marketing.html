Diagnosis

Why Most Social Media Marketing Doesn’t Work

The problem most UK businesses have with social media is not a lack of activity but no structure behind the activity. Content gets produced, platforms get managed, reports get sent and the business sees little to no commercial return because nobody connected the social media work to an actual business objective in the first place.

  1. 01

    No clear strategy behind the content

    Most social media management operates from a content calendar. It tells you what to post and when. But, if we follow a strategy it defines who you are targeting, what you need them to think or do, which platforms will reach them most effectively, and how social media connects to your wider marketing and sales activity.

  2. 02

    The wrong platforms for the business goals

    Platform selection is often based on assumption rather than audience data. A B2B company investing heavily in Instagram while their buyers spend their time on LinkedIn. An e-commerce brand absent from the platform where their customer demographic is most active. Choosing platforms based on what feels familiar, rather than where the opportunity actually exists, is one of the most common and most costly errors in social media management services.

  3. 03

    Creative that fills space rather than generates demand

    Branded content that looks consistent is not the same as content that performs. Much of what passes for social media management, templated graphics, recycled captions, low-effort video, satisfies the requirement to post without doing anything to build brand perception or drive commercial action. Creative direction on social media needs to be deliberate: built around audience psychology, platform behaviour, and a specific intended outcome.

  4. 04

    No connection between social activity and business results

    Paid social media directs users to landing pages that fail to convert. Organic content is produced without alignment to the sales funnel. Reporting focuses on visibility metrics without linking activity to revenue outcomes. In this structure, social media functions in isolation, which removes clarity from performance and limits the ability to optimise effectively.

At 360 Web Solutions, our social media marketing services are built around solving each of these problems directly.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions About Social Media Agency London

Scope, platforms, pricing and pacing — the questions UK businesses ask most often before engaging.

01 What is included in social media management UK?

Strategy development, content planning, content creation, platform management, paid campaign setup, audience targeting, and performance reporting.

02 Do I need organic social and paid social together?

Yes — organic builds credibility and audience trust, while paid social ensures reach and scalability. Both are required for consistent performance.

03 Which social media platforms are right for my business?

Platform selection depends on your target audience, industry, and objectives. B2B usually performs on LinkedIn, while B2C often performs better on platforms like Instagram or TikTok.

04 How much does social media marketing cost in the UK?

Costs vary based on scope, platforms, and campaign complexity. Typical agency retainers range from £1,000 to £5,000+ per month, excluding ad spend.

05 How long does it take to see results?

Initial performance indicators can appear within 4 to 8 weeks. Measurable business outcomes typically develop over 2 to 3 months with consistent optimisation.

06 Can social media help generate leads and sales?

Yes — when structured correctly, social media can drive targeted traffic, capture leads, and support direct sales through both organic and paid campaigns.

07 Do you create the content as well as manage campaigns?

Yes — content creation and campaign management are handled together to ensure consistency in messaging and alignment with performance goals.

08 What’s the difference between organic and paid social?

Organic social focuses on unpaid content and audience engagement. Paid social uses advertising budgets to reach targeted audiences and drive specific actions.

09 Do you offer white-label social media services UK?

Yes — white-label services allow agencies to outsource social media management while delivering the work under their own brand.
