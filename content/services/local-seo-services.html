What Are Local SEO Services?

Local SEO services are digital solutions that are integrated in order to optimise your business’s website for local-based searches. These services help your business rank on Google Maps, organic search queries and local pack results linked to specific geographic areas.

Traditional SEO tries to get your website to rank for big, broad searches. Local SEO works a little differently—it targets people looking for businesses in a specific area. Searches like “SEO agency near me” or “search engine optimisation Birmingham.” Those rely on local SEO tricks to show the right results to people nearby.

Frequently Asked Questions

01How long does local SEO take to work?

In general, businesses experience some success within 3-6 months; however, for more competitive results, it can take 6-12 months. The time frame can vary based on local competition levels, site authority, Google My Business profile optimisation, reviews, citations, and backlinks.

02How much do local SEO services cost in the UK?

The cost of local SEO services varies depending on your local market’s competitiveness, the number of localities, and the necessary work done on your website. In most cases, the cost ranges between £500 to £5,000 per month.

03How does local SEO differ from SEO?

Local SEO focuses on ranking a business in a specific geographic area, utilising Google Business Profile and localised keywords to drive foot traffic. Traditional SEO targets a broader, national, or global audience to boost general website authority.

04Can local SEO help me rank on Google Maps?

Yes, local SEO is the primary way to rank higher on Google Maps. By optimising your Google Business Profile (GBP), generating local reviews, and building local citations, you increase your chances of appearing in the top 3 results (the "Map Pack"). This boosts visibility, leading to more clicks, calls, and visits.

05Is local SEO better than paid ads?

In the epic battle of paid ads vs. local SEO, there’s no clear winner. The choice between the two depends on your business goals, budget, and patience. Paid Ads are a quick and direct route to visibility with instant results, whereas local SEO is a long-term investment in organic growth with lasting benefits.

06Do you offer local SEO services in London?

360 web solutions offers local SEO services across various cities in the UK, one of them being London. Our key local SEO services available in London include Google profile optimisation, regional keyword research, local content strategy, and link building.

07Do reviews affect local SEO?

Yes, reviews significantly affect local SEO. There’s a direct relation between reviews and your business’s visibility on Google Maps. Platforms like Google and Facebook are trustworthy for local users. Therefore, positive reviews on such platforms help build trust with potential customers and allow search engines to rank your business on top and regard it as reputable.

08Is local SEO worth it for small businesses?

Yes, for small businesses, local SEO is the primary source of expanding business operations. Local SEO for small businesses means showing up on Google Maps and local search results, with satisfactory reviews, opening hours, and your contact details right there. It’s a great way to connect with people who are already searching for the services or products you are selling.
