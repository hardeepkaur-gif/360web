Search & AI

SEO Content Built for Search Engines and AI-Driven Results

Search behaviour has shifted in a way that most content strategies haven’t caught up with yet. ChatGPT now holds 17.1% of all digital search volume, and Google’s AI Overview appears on over 80% of informational queries. Even when a page ranks, zero-click searches mean fewer users are actually arriving at your site.

Ranking is no longer enough on its own. Content now needs to be discoverable across traditional search results and extractable by AI platforms that synthesise answers rather than display links.

At 360 Web Solutions, we build SEO content that works across both environments. For traditional search, that means on-page structure, keyword targeting, and internal linking built around what Google rewards. For AI search, we apply Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO) and Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) — structuring content so AI models like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google’s AI Overview can extract and cite it directly.

In practice, GEO means each key question on a page is followed by a concise 2 to 3 sentence answer written specifically for AI extraction. This is what gets your business cited in generated responses rather than buried beneath them.

The goal hasn’t changed. Visibility, qualified traffic, and conversion. What has changed is how content needs to be built to achieve it.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions About SEO Content Writing Services

01 How is SEO content writing different from regular copywriting?

Regular content focuses on readability, while SEO content writing is built to rank and perform. It combines writing with technical SEO, keyword targeting, and content structure to improve search visibility and user engagement.

02 How long does SEO content take to rank?

SEO content starts showing results within 3 to 6 months. It depends on competition and keyword difficulty. Performance improves over time with consistent optimisation and content updates.

03 Can you improve existing website content instead of writing new pages?

Yes, SEO content audits and optimisation can improve rankings by refining keyword focus, updating outdated information, and restructuring content for better alignment with search intent and on-page SEO signals.

04 Do you write content for AI search and tools like ChatGPT?

Yes, content is structured for AI-driven platforms such as ChatGPT using approaches like Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO) and Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO), improving visibility in featured snippets and AI-generated results.

05 Which types of content do you provide?

SEO content writing services include website content, blog articles, landing pages, product descriptions, and content optimisation. Each type is aligned with keyword strategy and business goals.

06 How do you measure the success of SEO content?

Performance is measured through rankings, organic traffic, engagement metrics, and conversions. Additional indicators include visibility in featured snippets, indexing improvements, and presence in AI-driven search results.
