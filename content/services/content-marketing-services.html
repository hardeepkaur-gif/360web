FAQs

Content Marketing FAQs

01 What are content marketing services?

Content marketing services are end-to-end digital programmes where businesses planning, creating, publishing and analysing the content that attract your ideal customers. The main objective is to build audience trust, answer user queries, and generate organic leads for businesses.

02 How does content marketing generate leads?

Content marketing puts your business in front of buyers while they research their problem. Relevant content establishes trust even before they contact you, and clearly defined actions on every page turn that trust into leads. At 360, we strategise content at each step of that process.

03 How do you measure content marketing ROI?

We monitor your business concerns: leads, sales, and which pages bring them. We also provide reports on ranking, organic traffic, and AI search visibility. Every report provides insight into what content works and where we’ll change next.

04 How involved do I need to be with a content marketing agency?

Not too much. We begin with a 30-minute discovery session, and then you approve the strategy. After that, you approve the content before it goes live. We handle all other steps, including planning, publishing, distribution, and reporting.

05 How much do content marketing services cost?

Content marketing companies in the UK price by scope, so costs depend on your goals, channels, and content volume. A plan covering strategy, SEO content, email, and distribution costs more than blogs alone. At 360, we’ll recommend a plan that fits your goals and budget. Book a free content review for a clear quote.

06 What’s the difference between content marketing, SEO content writing, and copywriting?

Content marketing is the full strategy, from planning to distribution and reporting. SEO content writing creates blogs and how-to guides that rank for buyer searches. Copywriting turns website and landing page visitors into enquiries. We offer all three, so they work together, not in silos.

07 Should I hire a content marketing agency or do it in-house?

In-house works best if you have a strategist, writer, SEO specialist, and analyst on the team. An agency gives you all those skills without hiring each role separately. You also get a consistent plan, even when your team is busy.

08 Can content marketing work alongside SEO and PPC?

Yes, content marketing works best along with SEO and PPC. Content gives SEO more pages to rank while giving PPC stronger landing pages. Paid data also show which topics convert, which helps guide the next strategy plan.
