Why Most Google Ads Campaigns Underperform and How We Fix It?

Most businesses running Google Ads aren’t failing because the platform doesn’t work; in fact, they are failing because the campaigns are set up in ways that quietly drain budget without anyone flagging it. Here are the three main causes:

Broad Match Keywords Are Draining Your Budget

Unmanaged keywords are one of the sources of wasted spend in Google Ads campaign management. When campaigns rely too heavily on broad match without an optimised keyword list, your ads start appearing for searches that have nothing to do with your business.

Effective Google Ads management means building and continuously updating a negative keyword list from the very first day. We review search term reports weekly, not monthly, which allows us to catch irrelevant traffic before it compounds into a significant budget loss.

Clicks Without Conversion Means Ad and Landing Page Problem

Getting clicks is only half the job. If your landing page doesn’t match the intent of the ad that brought someone there, those clicks become expensive dead ends. Landing page relevance also feeds directly into your quality score, which is Google’s measure of how useful your ad and destination are to the searcher.

A poor quality score means you’re paying more per click and appearing lower in the results than competitors with smaller bids but greater relevance. Our Google Ads management service includes a landing page audit as standard. We identify the gaps between ad copy and page content that are costing you conversions, and give you specific, actionable recommendations to fix them.

Monthly PDF Reports Tell You Nothing Actionable

The usual practice of agencies is to send a monthly PDF with impressions, clicks and CTR. They might not be explaining clearly whether any of those clicks turned into enquiries, which campaigns are pulling their weight, and what needs to change. Reporting that stops at traffic metrics is not what we call reporting. It’s just a distraction from a real conversation.

We ensure that every client working with us at 360 Web Solutions gets weekly performance stats, live dashboard access, and a monthly review in simple English that answers three main points: what worked, what changed, and what is the next step.

Performance Max (PMax) Campaign Management
Performance Max is Google’s newest campaign type and the most widely misunderstood. PMax runs across search, display, YouTube, Gmail, maps and Discover from a single campaign using Google’s automation feature. But automation without proper audience signal inputs and asset management produces poor results. Our approach in PMax includes building strong audience signals from your already present customer data, classifying asset groups for each product category or service and running PMax alongside search campaigns rather than replacing them. Our team then monitors placement reports and excludes non-converting placements to keep spend focused.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

01 How much does Google Ads management cost in the UK?

Management fee typically ranges from £500 to £2000+ per month, depending on campaign complexity and the number of active campaigns. Some agencies charge 10–20% of your monthly ad spend, but we charge a flat fee. It’s important to ask upfront whether conversion tracking setup, landing page work, and ad copy creation are included because these are often billed separately elsewhere.

02 How long does it take to see results from Google Ads?

Search campaigns can generate traffic within 24–48 hours of going live. Meaningful optimisation data typically takes four to eight weeks to accumulate. The first 30 days are about targeting baseline issues and fixing structural errors, then comes the point where CPA improvements compound as smart bidding models learn from clean conversion data.

03 Do I own my Google Ads account if I leave the agency?

Yes, and you must verify this thing before signing anything. A reliable and reputable Google Ads management company will always request manager-level (MCC) access to an account you own, not build campaigns inside the account they control. If you plan to leave, you should be able to remove access and keep all campaign history, audience lists and conversion data intact.

04 How do I know if my current Google Ads agency is doing a good job?

If conversion tracking is properly configured in GA4, search term reports are reviewed, and negative keywords are added regularly, CPA is trending down over time rather than staying flat, and there’s direct account access for you at all times, then it’s a sign that your Google ads agency is putting in the right amount of effort. But if your agency discourages you from accessing your own account, that is one of the major red flags in Google Ads account management.

05 Should I manage Google Ads myself or hire an agency?

Google Ads management on your own can work for low-spend campaigns. Beyond that, the combination of match type complexity, smart bidding strategy, audience layering, feed management for shopping, and the time required for weekly optimisation makes professional Google Ads management a more cost-effective option for businesses.

06 What is Performance Max, and should I be running it?

Performance Max is Google’s automated cross-channel campaign type covering Search, YouTube, Maps, Discover, and Gmail. It can deliver strong results, but only when audience signals are properly configured. It works best alongside Search Campaigns and not as a replacement.
