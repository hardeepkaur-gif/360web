FAQs

Product Development Services FAQs

01 What are product development services?

Product development services take a business idea and build it into a digital product people pay for and use. At 360 Web Solutions, one team handles discovery, design, build and launch. We stay involved after release, fixing issues and adding what users ask for.

02 What is the difference between product development and software development?

Software development services cover one part of product development: writing and testing the code to an agreed brief. Product development goes wider. We test the idea, the users and the business case before anything is built. And we stay on after launch.

03 How long does software product development take?

Most MVPs take [X–X] weeks. A fuller product, with more features and integrations, usually needs [X–X] months once discovery is complete. Your response time counts as well. Quick feedback from your side keeps sprints moving, and discovery gives you a stage-by-stage schedule before the build.

04 How much do product development services cost in the UK?

Scope drives the price more than anything else. A focused MVP sits at the lower end; a multi-user platform with complex integrations costs considerably more. Discovery gives you a scoped estimate. Be careful with very low quotes, which tend to grow once rework begins.

05 Who owns the code and intellectual property?

You do. Under UK law, the person who creates commissioned work owns it unless ownership is transferred in writing. So we put it in the contract. Code and IP pass to you on [final payment], and you can see the repository from the first sprint.

06 Do I need a detailed specification before we start?

No. Most clients arrive with an idea, a problem to solve or a rough sketch on paper. That’s enough. In discovery, we agree on users, features and priorities together, then turn them into a brief you sign off on.

07 Do you develop physical products?

No. We only build digital products: software platforms, SaaS, web apps, and mobile apps. For a physical item, such as a device or packaging, an industrial design or engineering firm is the right partner. Need a companion app for it? We can build that.
