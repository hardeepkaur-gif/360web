FAQs

SEO Services FAQs

01 What are SEO services?

SEO services improve a website’s visibility in search engines and AI answers. It involves technical fixes, on-page optimization, content management, local search and link building. The goal is for the right people to find the business at the right time, and ultimately increase conversions.

02 How much do SEO services cost in the UK?

Most UK businesses pay between £500 and £2,000 a month. Local campaigns cost less, while competitive national or ecommerce campaigns cost more. Website size and business goals are another pricing factor. We are transparent about our rates and the deliverables upfront.

03 How long does SEO take to show results?

Early movement can appear in 90 days, with compound growth over 6 to 12 months. Timeline reflects your starting point and market competition. Our free audit will show a realistic estimate for your site.

04 Do you lock clients into long contracts?

We work on monthly contracts, so you stick around because there are results. You can leave at any time. You keep your content, data, or accounts, and we won’t stop you.

05 Can you guarantee #1 rankings?

No, and beware of the agencies that promise a top rank. Search results are outside anybody’s control. We commit to a clear strategy, transparent reporting and agreed targets, showing you progress against them every month.

06 Does SEO still matter with AI search?

Yes. Ranking well means being authoritative in your niche, and AI cites authority. Strong SEO helps your visibility on AI tools like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews. We structure your content so search engines and AI tools can understand and recommend your brand.
