AI explained

What Is an AI Consultancy?

An AI Consultancy service is a specialist advisory and implementation service. This service helps businesses understand where artificial intelligence can create value, and which tools or approaches are appropriate for their situation. It explains how to implement AI safely and efficiently.

It is totally different from purchasing AI software (which is a product decision) and from an AI marketing agency (which manages an AI campaign using AI tools). AI consultancy services provide strategic judgment and technical implementation to make AI work within your business.

Consultant role

What Does an AI Consultant Do?

An AI consultant’s job is to understand your business problem. This means identifying where your idea is good enough to support automation and where AI could genuinely reduce costs and increase revenue.

In practical terms, a good AI consultant will do:

  • Audit your current workflow to find where AI can create real results
  • Map your data quality because poor data quality is the number one cause of AI projects failing
  • Recommend specific tools and systems based on your stack and budget
  • Build the fully documented solution and handover, so that your team can actually use it
  • Train your team members so the investment does not sit idle

The value of AI consultancy is not knowing how to connect the HubSpot to LLMs. It is important to know when to do it, what breaks in production, and to be honest when a simpler solution serves you better.

Avoiding AI Hype and Wasted Spend

88% of companies use AI in different forms, but only 6% report a clear financial return. The main reason is not the technology; it’s a poor framing. Not every Zapier workflow is an AI transformation. Not every chatbot needs to be built from scratch. Most UK businesses are better off with a properly configured off-the-shelf platform at a fraction of the cost.

Ask one question before committing to any solution: What specific problem does this actually solve? If the answer is not clear and contains phrases like “AI-powered efficiency” without a defined metric. This is a clear signal for you to pause. The other half of the problem is what happens after the delivery.

Properly defined AI consultation companies have outcomes that can be measured before the process starts. They provide full documentation and a clear handover to your team. They provide something that does not require the proper ongoing presence for its maintenance. The 2025 report by McKinsey shows that 73% of AI projects never move beyond the pilot stage due to a lack of clear outcomes from the start.

When We Tell You Not to Use AI

Some businesses are not ready for AI; it’s not a failure, it’s a good step to know before investment. We will recommend that you stop AI implementation if you have:

  • If your data is unreliable and siloed, you must stop, because AI does not fix poor data; it scales it and leads to poor outcomes.

  • If your processes are not documented, AI automation makes it break faster and at greater cost.

  • If your team is not ready.

  • If a simpler and better-designed process, basic automation, or a £30/month tool would solve your problem. We will tell you clearly without apology.

FAQs

AI Consultancy FAQs

01 Does my business need AI?

Not at all. Some businesses require better processes, cleaner data, or simple automation. AI consultancy services can fulfil those requirements too. You can use our free AI readiness assessment to answer these questions at no cost.

02 What is the difference between AI consultancy and AI automation?

AI consultancy diagnoses what should be built; AI automation typically executes what you ask for. The main difference is strategic diagnosis versus tool deployment.

03 Can AI help small businesses and SMEs?

Yes, when the application is right. According to a 2025 survey, 35% of UK SMEs are actively using AI. Successful small-business use cases often include customer support automation, lead qualification, content workflows, and process automation. It starts with the correct use case.

04 How much does an AI consultancy service cost in the UK?

AI readiness assessments can start from a few hundred to a few thousand pounds. Strategy and roadmap engagements often range from £5,000–£15,000; full implementation from roughly £8,000 up to around £100,000 depending on scope; specialist boutique firms often quote around £500–£900 per day, with independent consultants from about £580 per day. See the full pricing section above.

05 Do you provide generative AI consultancy services?

Yes. Our generative AI consultancy covers LLM integration, prompt engineering, RAG systems, and AI agent work — core parts of implementation. We help you apply generative AI to specific, justified use cases.

06 Can you train our team to use AI?

Yes. Team training and adoption are standard on our engagements — deployed solutions are wasted if your team cannot use them. We run structured sessions tailored to your tools, workflows, and skill level.

07 Is AI consultancy safe for sensitive business data?

It should be — but it depends how the engagement is run. We include data security review, GDPR alignment, and AI governance as standard. We do not recommend setups that expose your data to unnecessary risk.

08 Do you offer AI consultancy services in London and across the UK?

Yes. We work with businesses across the UK, including London, Manchester, and Birmingham. Location is rarely a blocker for most engagements.

09 How long does an AI consultancy project take?

A readiness assessment usually takes one to two weeks. Strategy and roadmap work typically runs two to four weeks. Implementation can take a few weeks to several months depending on scope. We agree a clear timeline up front and work to it.
