Email Marketing Strategies
Email marketing starts with a proper strategy. First, we map your audience and define your campaign. Then we set specific and measurable KPIs. The next step is aligning your email programme with your SEO, social and website funnels.
Email Campaign Planning
Email campaign planning is an important part of driving consistent revenue. Ad hoc email sending is one of the important factors why UK businesses fail to rank. We create a structured calendar mapping successful promotional emails, seasonal campaigns, and automated flows. These sequences and a coherent strategy drive continuous revenue.
Email Design and Template
We build mobile-responsive designs and templates that align with your brand. These designs and templates can render perfectly across the major email clients, including Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail and mobile devices. Our accessibility-compliant HTML templates meet EAA 2025 standards, making sure it is readable by all subscribers, including those using assistive technologies.
Email Copywriting and Content
Open rates depend upon a good subject line. Body copy wins the revenue. Our specialist copywriters craft attention-grabbing lines, compelling preview text, and CTAs. Personalisation increases the open rate and improves the campaign.
Marketing Automation and Drip Campaigns
We build the automation flows that bring revenue. We build and optimise automation based on audience purchase history, behaviour and stage. These include welcome emails series for new subscribers, abandoned cart recoveries, reactivation campaigns, post-purchase emails, and seasonal promotions.
List Hygiene and Audience Segmentation
A disengaged list can damage a sender’s reputation and reduce deliverability across your entire programme. We maintain a clean list by removing inactive audiences and segmenting the rest on the basis of their purchase history, behaviour and engagement level. This results in higher open rates and better revenue per email.
A/B Testing and Optimisation
Our A/B testing covers subject lines, CTAs, personalised content, email layout, sender names and content length. Each test is structured and defines a specific success metric. Monthly reviews ensure your email programme performs better than the last month.
AI-Driven Personalisation and Send-Time Optimisation
We use AI-supported tools that enhance personalisation on a larger scale and optimise send times for individual subscribers. AI-powered tools ensure that subscribers receive emails at the right moment, when they are most likely to engage.
Monthly Insights and Email Analytics
We give a complete performance report every month covering your campaign revenue attribution, subscribers’ lifetime value trends, a list of growth metrics, and revenue per email. You will exactly know what is going on and what your email programme is generating.
CRM and ESP Integration

Best Email Marketing Platforms We Can Work With

Choosing an email marketing platform entirely depends on your business model, audience list, budget and automation needs. We are neutral when it comes to platforms, so we recommend it according to your needs.

Here are the platforms that we use more frequently in UK businesses, according to how they work and help in different sectors.

01

Mailchimp

Easy-to-use campaign design ideal for simple automation for e-commerce brands. Strong for cart abandonment, newsletter campaigns, and the go-to choice for many small UK businesses.

02

Klaviyo

Built for e-commerce and retail, driving conversion from behaviour-based data with advanced personalisation and segmentation across the customer journey.

03

Brevo (formerly Sendinblue)

A flexible, cost-effective option for transactional email and SMS campaigns — widely used by B2B brands and professional services.

04

HubSpot

Our preferred platform for B2B email with complex sales cycles, multiple stakeholders, and CRM-integrated data for multi-channel engagement.

05

ActiveCampaign

Best for SMBs and professional services needing multi-condition automation, deep segmentation, and streamlined customer journeys for maximum engagement.

06

Omnisend

Retail and e-commerce brands that need integrated email and SMS, time-sensitive promotions, and reliable abandoned cart recovery.

07

EmailOctopus

Cost-effective simple automation and high-volume sending — ideal for small businesses and hospitality.
