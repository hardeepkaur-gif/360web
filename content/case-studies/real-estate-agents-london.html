The Challenge

London real estate is one of the most competitive digital markets in the country. The businesses ranking for terms like “real estate agents London” included national brands with long-established search histories and link profiles many times the size of this one.

  • Domain Authority: 5 against competitors at 40–70+
  • Keyword difficulty: “Real estate agents London” — 68/100
  • Content gap: Competitors ranking with 1,800 to 3,000-word pages
  • Incomplete profile, few reviews, no citations
  • Technical debt: A full rebuild was needed before ranking was possible

Our Approach

A Five-Part Digital Programme Built to Fix Foundations First, Then Compete for Rankings

Before competing for high-difficulty terms, the site needed a technical and authority foundation. The programme covered five connected areas:

On-Page SEO

  • Rewrote titles, meta descriptions, and heading structure across all 35 pages
  • Mapped 200+ keywords to individual pages, including local geo-modified terms
  • Fixed technical issues: broken URLs, orphan pages, image optimisation, and schema markup

Off-Page SEO & Local Search

  • Fully optimised Google Business Profile with a structured review-generation programme
  • Built 50+ local citations and secured 30+ guest posts on property and landlord blogs
  • Ran PR outreach to earn editorial backlinks from UK property publications

WordPress Development

  • Rebuilt the site fully responsive, on-brand, and conversion-focused
  • Added a free valuation form, WhatsApp click-to-chat, and a tenant/landlord maintenance portal
  • Resolved every crawl error and enforced SSL sitewide

Content

  • Rewrote all core service pages to 1,500–2,500 words with proper structure and FAQs
  • Created 12 London area guides and published 24 blog posts on landlord and tenant topics
  • Added trust signals throughout: team bios, certifications, and client numbers

Social Media

  • Built a content calendar of 30 posts a month across seven platforms
  • Grew presence from ~250 total followers to ~6,700+, with all engagement managed within 24 hours

Reputation & Review Management

Protecting Trust Where It Matters Most

In local property search, reviews are often the first thing a prospective tenant or landlord checks before making contact, and a handful of unfair or fake reviews can quietly undo months of SEO progress by damaging trust at the exact moment a buyer is deciding who to call. For Real Estate Agents London, review management ran alongside the SEO and content programme rather than sitting apart from it.

  1. 1

    Monitoring

    Reviews across the Google Business Profile were tracked on an ongoing basis.

  2. 2

    Response & Resolution

    Structured processes were used to flag and address unfair or fake reviews where they appeared.

  3. 3

    Review Generation

    A structured email sequence was run to past clients, actively requesting genuine Google reviews.

The result: reviews grew from 4 to 413, at a 4.8★ average

Why This Worked

Foundations Before Rankings

The instinct in a competitive market like London property is to chase the biggest keywords first. We did the opposite. The site had almost no technical foundation, no content depth, and barely any authority. Competing for “real estate agents london” directly against national chains would have wasted months of effort with nothing to show for it.

Instead, the programme fixed the technical and trust foundations first: site health, structure, reviews, citations, then won the achievable local terms, and let authority compound outward from there. This is the principle that applies to any local business competing against bigger, longer-established rivals: you don’t out-market a national brand’s history; you out-execute them on the terms they’ve stopped paying attention to, and build upward from a position of genuine strength.
