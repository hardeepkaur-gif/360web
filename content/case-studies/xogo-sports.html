The Challenge

Winning the Comparison

It is worth being precise about the problem, because the wrong diagnosis leads to the wrong invoice. XOGO did not have a traffic problem. It had a presence in its categories, and it was appearing in the results buyers were looking at. What it did not have was a reason for the buyer to choose it once it appeared.

When a buyer sees two functionally identical products at similar prices, the decision is made on the cues that are visible without clicking: image quality, brand consistency, title clarity, and the seller’s credibility markers. The seller who loses that comparison has three options: cut the price, accept the lost sale, or fix the presentation. Only the third one compounds.

Our Approach

Rebuilding Presentation at the Exact Moment a Buyer Chooses

Our engagement model targeted the point of comparison itself: the half-second where a buyer decides between near-identical options.

  • Applied the XOGO® mark, a lion-crest identity, and one consistent art-direction standard across every listing

eBay Store Development

  • Rebuilt the store as a branded shopfront with navigation, dedicated sale view, and a follow mechanism to build an owned audience

Marketplace SEO

  • Optimised titles, item specifics, and dual category placement so listings surface across every relevant search path

Why This Worked

Winning the Half-Second Before the Click

Most sellers in a commodity category respond to weak sales the same way: drop the price. That’s the trap XOGO was heading into. Its products were competitive, but sitting in a comparison grid full of near-identical alternatives, the only thing left to compete on was price, and that path only ever gets more expensive.

The shift came from targeting a moment most sellers ignore entirely: the second or two a buyer spends scanning the results grid before they’ve even opened a listing. Branding, image consistency, and complete structured data were all aimed at that exact moment, winning the clicks already happening. It’s a principle worth remembering for any seller stuck in a price war: the fix usually is making the visibility you already have count.
