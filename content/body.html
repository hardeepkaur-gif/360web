Digital Marketing Agency London FAQs

Straight answers to the questions we hear most often from prospective clients.

01 What does a digital marketing agency do?

A digital marketing agency helps businesses grow online by connecting every channel that influences buying decisions. At 360 we don’t run these in silos — your SEO data feeds your PPC, your content supports organic, and your data drives every decision.

  • → SEO, PPC, paid social & content marketing
  • → Web design, development & CRO
  • → AI, automation & generative engine optimisation
  • → Transparent, real-time reporting on one dashboard
02 How do I choose the right digital marketing agency in the UK?

The right agency audits your position before proposing a solution — not after you’ve signed. We built our free strategy session around this, so you can evaluate us before committing.

  • → Audits before proposals, not pitch decks
  • → Proven results in your industry
  • → Transparent, real-time reporting you can access anytime
  • → One dedicated strategist, not a rotating account manager
03 Is it better to hire a digital marketing agency or build an in-house team?

An in-house team brings deep brand knowledge but a narrow market view, and hiring takes 4–8 weeks before anything starts. An agency brings multi-channel expertise, live market data, and specialist tools from day one — at a fraction of the cost of a full senior team.

04 How long does digital marketing take to show results?

Results depend on your starting point — which is exactly what we map in your free strategy session. Our clients average 142% organic traffic growth in the first six months.

  • → PPC: qualified leads within days
  • → SEO: meaningful movement within 90 days
  • → Compounding: growth accelerates over 6–12 months
  • → CRO: revenue lift from existing traffic inside 30 days
05 Do I need SEO, PPC, and paid social running together?

You don’t always need it from day one, but the businesses that grow fastest run channels that share data. SEO tells you what your buyers search; PPC tests what converts fastest; paid social builds the audience that makes both cheaper over time. That’s the 360 loop.

06 Can AI replace a digital marketing agency?

AI can generate content and surface anomalies faster than any human. What it cannot do is build the strategy, understand your market, make judgement calls when the data contradicts the brief, or take accountability for results. We use AI to multiply our team — not replace the thinking.

07 Which industries do you specialise in?

We built specialist teams around the sectors where marketing strategies genuinely differ. We also selectively work with businesses outside these when the fit is right.

  • → Real Estate — from first search to signed contract
  • → E-commerce — ROAS and revenue, not rankings alone
  • → SaaS / B2B — CAC, LTV & pipeline from pre-launch to Series B
  • → Healthcare & Fintech — compliant, trust-led growth
08 What happens in the first 30 days of working with 360?

The first month is deliberately front-loaded — we find the gaps, build the plan, and launch while other agencies are still onboarding.

  • → Week 1 · Audit — SEO, paid, content, technical & competitive
  • → Week 2 · Strategy — bespoke channel plan, budget & 90-day targets
  • → Week 3 · Build — creatives, briefs, tracking & dashboard
  • → Week 4 · Live — campaigns active, metrics tracking in real time
